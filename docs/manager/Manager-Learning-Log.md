## Entries

### 2026-09-07 (week 1)

Evidence: live Salesforce query 7 Sep, 4,612 open Cases org-wide, 18 with RecordType Zeus. The figure "4,047 Cases in New" had been carried since 8 Aug in birdlife-core, open-items.csv and the narrative to the Executive Director as the ICT helpdesk backlog. Asana IT Operations Project Plan: 85 of 112 open tasks undated, 3 items blocked 33 days on a Mathew decision, Mathew personally holding 31 tasks with 30 undated.

Learning: a number carried without its scope becomes a story about the wrong thing. The ICT backlog never existed at that size; the programme queues do, and they are not ICT's to clear. Separately, the board shows the manager holding the least dated work and the oldest blocked decisions, which is the reverse of what the capability ladder asks of the team.

Habit: every count leaves the team with its scope clause in the same sentence. Every Blocked item older than 14 days gets a decision slot in the same week. The manager's own tasks are dated first, not last.

Stuck when: the 14 Sep review shows Blocked-on-Mathew at 0, Mathew's undated at 0, and the doctrine query carries the Zeus WHERE clause.
