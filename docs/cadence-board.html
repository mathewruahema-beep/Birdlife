          The rules

          1. Two hours a day, four fixed slots. The slots are the improvement; everything else is the job.
          2. One fix to verified per day. Observed in the system, not scripted. Tier 2 counts when the admin has run it and you re-read the result.
          3. Learn the same day. The 16:30 slot exists to write the skill edit. Unwritten fixes get rediscovered in three months.
          4. No new routines. Budget is 12 of 12. A recurring 09:15 task is a change to an existing routine.
          5. Three at a time. The weekly plan names three fixes. The fourth is noise.
          6. Impact on people on every line. No people line, no proposal.
          Source of truth: docs/daily-improvement-schedule.md and docs/improvement-log.md in the Birdlife repo. Times are Melbourne local (AEST until 4 Oct 2026, then AEDT; the routines follow an hour later until the DST one-shot re-derives them). Baselines are the last live reads recorded in the repo and skills; the first Friday re-reads them all.
