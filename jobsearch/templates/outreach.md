# Outreach templates

Send within 24 hours of applying. Four to six lines. One proof point, one ask. No attachments
on first contact. Replace the bracketed parts; keep the rest.

## Hiring manager, LinkedIn message (after applying)

Subject or first line: Applied for [Title], one thing worth knowing

Hi [First name],

I applied for the [Title] role today. One thing that may not be obvious from the form: I have
led IT for organisations like [theirs, in their words] before, most recently [one-line proof
point with a number]. I work remotely from Melbourne on AEST and have run distributed teams
across Australia, the USA, the UK and India.

If it is useful, I can walk you through how I would approach the first 90 days. Either way,
thank you for considering it.

Mathew Hema
mathew.rua.hema@gmail.com

transformation, NFP and financial services, remote from Melbourne.

Thanks either way.

Mathew
