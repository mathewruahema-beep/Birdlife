# Negotiation and structure

## Before you give a number

Ask: "What is the range you have approved for this role?" Most Australian employers will
tell you. If they will not: "My floor for a role at this scope is [floor] base plus super;
the right number depends on the remit and the team, which I would like to understand
first."

## Your floors (from profile.json, revise as the market tells you)

- Full-time employee: AUD 160,000 base plus super. Realistic band for Head of IT at 150 to
  500 staff in Australia: 170,000 to 220,000 plus super. CIO title at NFPs: 180,000 to
  230,000. Financial services and insurance pay more; NFPs pay less and offer salary
  packaging (roughly 15,900 a year tax-free, worth 5,000 to 7,000 in take-home).
- Contract: AUD 900 a day floor; 1,000 to 1,300 for CIO or Head of IT scope.
- Fractional: AUD 8,000 to 15,000 a month for one to two days a week; 90-day embedded
  stabilisation engagements 50,000 to 60,000.

## Conversion between structures

Day rate is roughly base salary divided by 220 working days, times 1.25 to 1.4 to cover
leave, super, insurance, gaps and risk. AUD 180,000 base is about AUD 1,050 to 1,150 a day.
Going the other way, a 1,000 a day contract is worth about 160,000 to 175,000 as a salary.

## Employer of record offers (overseas companies)

- Ask which EOR (Deel, Remote.com, Oyster, Papaya). You will be their Australian employee.
- Get the salary in AUD, or the exchange rate fixed at signing and reviewed annually.
- Confirm super is paid on top at the Australian statutory rate, not deducted from the number.
- Confirm leave (four weeks plus public holidays, Australian), notice period, and who owns
  equipment.
- Ask what happens to the contract if the company leaves the EOR.

## Contractor offers

- ABN sole trader or your own company. Personal services income rules can treat company
  income as yours; get accounting advice before you structure anything.
- Professional indemnity and public liability insurance will usually be required; price it
  before you quote.
- Payment terms 14 days. Invoice weekly or fortnightly for long contracts.

## The one ask

Negotiate once, on the thing that matters most. Say it plainly, then stop:
"The offer is close. If you can move the base to [number], I will accept today." Silence.
If they cannot move base, trade: an extra week of leave, a review at six months tied to
named outcomes, a title change, or the remote arrangement written into the contract.

## Before you sign

- Written offer matches the verbal one.
- Remote arrangement, working window and travel expectation are in the contract, not an
  email.
- Probation length and what ends it.
- Restraint clauses you can live with.
- You have spoken to at least one person who would report to you.
