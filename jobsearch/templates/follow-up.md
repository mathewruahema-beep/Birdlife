# Follow-up templates

Rules: day 7, day 14, then stop. Each message is shorter than the last. Each adds one thing.
Never say "just checking in".

## Day 7

Subject: [Title] application, Mathew Hema

Hi [First name],

I applied for the [Title] role on [date] and wanted to add one thing that was not in the
letter: [one new proof point or a short view on something in their posting].

Could you tell me where you are in the process and when you expect to shortlist?

Mathew

## Day 14

Subject: Re: [Title] application

Hi [First name],

Still very interested in the [Title] role. I am progressing other conversations and would
rather not miss this one, so if there is a timeline you can share it would help me plan.

Mathew

## After a screening call or interview (same day)

Subject: Thank you, [Title]

Hi [First name],

Thank you for the time today. Two things stayed with me: [specific thing they said], and
[the problem they described]. On the second, [one sentence on how you have handled the same
thing before, with a number].

I am keen to continue. What is the next step and when?

Mathew

## After a presentation or case round

Hi [First name],

Thank you for the panel's time. If it helps the decision, here is the one-page version of the
90-day plan I presented, with the two questions [name] raised answered in the margin.

I would take this role. Let me know what you need from me.

Mathew

## Withdrawal (keep the relationship)

Hi [First name],

I am withdrawing from the [Title] process. [One honest sentence: I have accepted another
role / the arrangement described differs from the posting / the timing does not work.] I
appreciated the way you ran the process and would be glad to talk again if something
changes.

Mathew

## Referee heads-up

Hi [First name],

I have listed you as a referee for a [Title] role at [Company]. They may call in the next
two weeks. The role is [one line]. The things they care most about are [two things]. If you
would rather not this time, tell me and I will swap you out, no problem.

Thank you.

Mathew
