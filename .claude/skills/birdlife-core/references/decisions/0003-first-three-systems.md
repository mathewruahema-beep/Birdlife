# ADR 0003: Salesforce, Asana and Office 365 as the first connected systems

- **Status:** Accepted, since superseded in scope by ADR 0006
- **Date:** August 2026
- **Decided by:** Mathew Hema

## Decision
Connect Salesforce, Asana and Office 365 first. Primary outcome: ICT operational
efficiency. Platform: Claude with connectors.

## Why these three
They cover the helpdesk (Salesforce Cases), the work tracker (Asana) and identity plus
communications (M365). Together they span the full path of a typical ticket.

## Note
The connected estate has since grown well beyond three. See ADR 0006.
