## Register C — structured long-form

### Incident/Board-adjacent (Quest data breach reply to Kim)

> Hi Kim,
>
> Thanks for forwarding this so quickly.
>
> Short answer: nothing of ours has been breached, and I would hold the note
> to directors until early this afternoon, so we send one accurate message
> rather than two.
>
> **What I have checked so far**
> [...] No BirdLife system is involved. This is Quest's incident, through one
> of their suppliers. [...] The practical risk here is a well-informed
> phishing or fake invoice attempt, not identity theft.
>
> **On telling the Board**
>
> I agree they should be told. Two changes to how.
>
> First, sequencing. [...] If you brief directors on another organisation's
> incident before we have the facts, and Quest then revises its assessment,
> we must correct ourselves to the Board. That costs us more than a few
> hours delay does.
>
> Second, channel and content. It should go from the Board Secretary as one
> short note, not a forward of the Quest email, and it should contain no
> links at all. The reason is uncomfortable but real: a message telling
> people to expect communications about a breach is exactly the opening a
> scammer wants.

### Structural notes

- Section headings he actually used: "My error, and why it happened" / "Two
  other things worth saying back" / "What we owe you, and when" / "What I
  have checked so far" / "On telling the Board" / "What we actually say" /
  "What our members actually see". Plain descriptions, sometimes with a
  clause of judgement built in.
- Numbered answers open with a bold sentence that both labels and asserts:
  "**4. Family member identity and matching. You have read our intent
  correctly and this is the one that needs a decision rather than a
  confirmation.**"
- Audience management is explicit: "Copying Ben, James and Karishma in for
  this part."
- Negotiation emails verify line by line and ask numbered questions with
  the maths shown ("I can see that the Experience Cloud login uplift from
  $0.58 to $2.52 per login per month...").
