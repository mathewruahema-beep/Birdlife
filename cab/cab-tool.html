The process, on one page

Every production change gets a record here before it happens — or, for emergencies, the same day. Risk decides scrutiny: most changes should pass with an async thumbs-up, not a meeting. The full process lives in the repository at cab/CAB-PROCESS.md.

Change types

TypeApproval
Standard — pre-approved catalogue procedureNone. Log it and go.
Normal — everything elseBy risk score, below
Emergency — restore service / close a security hole nowVerbal OK from CAB Chair, log same day, retro review at next CAB

Risk = Impact × Likelihood

ScoreRiskWho approves
1–2LowAny one other CAB member, async
3–4MediumCAB Chair + one member, async or next meeting
6–9HighCAB meeting, quorum of 3 incl. Chair, business stakeholder invited
  • Anything touching payments, donations or member joins in production is minimum Medium.
  • A change with no rollback plan is not approvable.

Roles

  • CAB Chair: Mathew Hema — runs meetings, tie-breaks, approves emergencies.
  • CAB members: Andrew Dunn, Keith Tsui, Nina Lewis.
  • Business stakeholders join for High-risk changes to their domain.

Freeze windows

No Medium/High changes to the affected domain during these windows without CAB-meeting approval. The form warns automatically.

Cadence

CAB meets fortnightly for 30 minutes, appended to an existing ICT catch-up. Between meetings, Low and Medium changes move by async approval here. In Asana, Ready for Deployment = approved & scheduled; Hypercare = implemented, in verification before Close.
