### 

Satin Bowerbird P*tilonorhynchus violaceu*s and Grey Currawong *Strepera versicolor* Eating Feijoa *Acca sellowian*a Petals

 Lawrence E. Conole
pp. 121-124

### Abstract

This paper reports on the eating of flower petals of the introduced Feijoa (Pineapple Guava) *Acca sellowiana* by the Satin Bowerbird* Ptilonorhynchus violaceus* and Grey Currawong *Strepera versicolor*, in the Otway Ranges, south-western Victoria, in December 2003. A brief review of the eating of Feijoa petals by birds, and the role it plays in pollination in the plant's native and introduced range, is also included.
