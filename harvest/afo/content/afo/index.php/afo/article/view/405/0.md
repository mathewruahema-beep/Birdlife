### 

Prion wreck at beaches at Wreck Bay, New South Wales, includes the Broad-billed Prion

 Martin Schulz
p. 107-108

### Abstract

A wreck of Broad-billed Prions *Pachyptila vittata* at Wreck Bay, New South Wales, beaches is documented. It followed a large-scale wreck of the same species in New Zealand a week earlier.
