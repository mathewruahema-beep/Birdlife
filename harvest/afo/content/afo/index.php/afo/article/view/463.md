### 

Possible Range Expansion of Wintering Pied Harriers *Circus melanoleucos* into the Southern Hemisphere

 MUHAMMAD IQBAL, GIYANTO -
p. 33-34

### Abstract

Three records of the Pied Harrier *Circus melanoleucos* in Sumatra, Indonesia, during the last decade (1999–2009) suggest that the recent status of this species in Sumatra is one of a possible regular visitor rather than a vagrant. These records are also a possible southern expansion of the wintering range of this species.
