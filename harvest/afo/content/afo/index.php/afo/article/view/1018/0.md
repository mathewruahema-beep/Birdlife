### 

Observations at the Nest of a Painted Snipe *Rostratula benghalensis *in South-east Queensland

 C J Leach, C G Lloyd, H B Hines
P. 15-19

### Abstract

Some observations are reported on the behaviour of a male Painted Snipe *Rostratula benghalensis *at a nest in South-east Queensland during January 1986. Timing of breeding is also discussed in relation to seasonal flooding of swamps in eastern Australia.
