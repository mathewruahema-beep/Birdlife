### 

Second nest of the Bougainville Honeyeater *Stresemannia bougainvillei*, with the first description of a nest in situ, and some new findings regarding its elevational distribution

 Jan-Uwe Schmidt

### Abstract

A nest and hatchling of the Bougainville Honeyeater *Stresemannia bougainvillei* found in situ in the wild are described. This is only the second nest described by biologists in the literature, and presents new altitudinal distribution for the species.
