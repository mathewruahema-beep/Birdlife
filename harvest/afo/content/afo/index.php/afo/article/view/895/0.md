### 

Parental Care and Feeding of Nestlings in the Mistletoebird *Dicaeum hirundinaceum*

 Betty Balingall
P. 174-177

### Abstract

The nest of a pair of Mistletoebirds *Dicaeum hirundinaceum *was observed on eight days during the 14 day nestling period in February 1987 near Oakey in inland south-east Queensland. Both parents fed the young and disposed of faecal sacs; only the female brooded. The young were first fed on arthropods only, then mistletoe fruits were introduced at 3-4 days old. Fruits and arthopods were fed approximately equally until the young were 6 days old, and thereafter approximately one in five meals consisted of arthropods; the young were fed approximately 10 times per hour.
