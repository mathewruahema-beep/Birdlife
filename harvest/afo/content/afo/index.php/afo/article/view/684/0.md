### 

Sight Records of a Black-headed Gull *Larus ridibundis* in the Northern Territory

 Nivien McCrie, Roger Jaensch
P. 87-92

### Abstract

This paper reports sight records of a Black-headed Gull *Lams ridibundus *in Darwin, Northern Territory. The record has been accepted by the Birds Australia Rarities Committee (Submission 237) and comprises the second record of the species in Australia, and the first from the Northern Territory.
