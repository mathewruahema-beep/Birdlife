### 

Square-tailed Kites Breeding on the Mid-north Coast of New South Wales

 Tony Bischoff, Hans Lutter, Stephen Debus
P. 233-240

### Abstract

The nests of two pairs of Square-tailed Kites *Lophoictinia isura* were observed over two breeding seasons on the mid-north coast of New South Wales in 1997 and 1998. The nests were in Blackbutt *Eucalyptus pilularis* forest beside the Pacific Highway, 18 km apart and separated by farmland. Nest 1 produced two fledglings in 1997, and failed at the egg stage in 1998. Nest 2 apparently reached the nestling stage in 1997 but then failed, and was not occupied in 1998. In 1999 no nests were found at either site, although single Kites were seen in the breeding season near nest 1. Incubation lasted about 41 days, and the post-fledging dependence period lasted almost two months. The Kites were not disturbed by highway traffic and roadworks below the nest. Breeding behaviour is described, and incidental dietary records are presented.
