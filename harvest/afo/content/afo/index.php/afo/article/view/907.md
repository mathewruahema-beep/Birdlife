### 

The Breeding Behaviour of a Brown Goshawk *Accipiter fasciatus didimus *Pair

 T Aumann
P. 156-163

### Abstract

The breeding behaviour of a Brown Goshawk *Accipiter fasciatus didimus *pair is described for the final three weeks of incubation and the first four weeks of the nestling period. The breeding behaviour of *didimus *in the tropics is compared with that previously described for *A. f fasciatus *in southern temperate Australia.
