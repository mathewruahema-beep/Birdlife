### 

Eastern Whipbirds *Psophodes olivaceus* Holding and Feeding on Sunflower Seeds

 JENNIFER SPRY
p. 42-44

### Abstract

This note describes the methods used by Eastern Whipbirds *Psophodes olivaceus* to hold, manipulate and eat Common Sunflower *Helianthus annuus* seeds (grey-striped variety), based in particular on the observation of a pair of birds in far south-eastern New South Wales.
