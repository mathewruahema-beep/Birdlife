### 

A mixed-species brood of wild Australian parrots (Eastern Rosella *Platycercus eximius* and Crimson Rosella* P. elegans*)— accident or brood-parasitism?

 Johanne M. Martens, Helena S. Stokes, Mathew L. Berg, Andrew T.D. Bennett
pp. 87-90

### Abstract

We report a novel case of a mixed-species brood in wild Australian parrots. Two Eastern Rosellas *Platycercus eximius* and one Crimson Rosella *P. elegans* were raised by a pair of Eastern Rosellas in the same nest-box in southern Victoria, Australia, in late 2016.
