### 

Seasonal Fluctuations in Waterbird Populations on Three Lakes near Camperdown, Victoria

 R Missen, B Timms
pp. 128-135

### Abstract

From three years of observations, 34 species of birds were recorded on Lake Purrumbete (fresh), 15 on Lake Bullenmerri (slightly saline) and 8 on Lake Gnotuk (very salty). The number of most species fluctuated regularly and a majority were most numerous in summer/autumn. During this period surrounding wetlands are dry, and so these deep lakes act as important refuges during dry times. The occurrence of each species with respect to salinity and season is discussed.
