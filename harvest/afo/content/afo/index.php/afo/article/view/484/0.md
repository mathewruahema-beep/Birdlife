### 

Breeding and Diet of the Little Eagle *Hieraaetus morphnoides* in Central Queensland

 KEITH D FISHER
p. 119-127

### Abstract

The breeding productivity and diet of up to three pairs of Little Eagles *Hieraaetus morphnoides* were monitored at Longreach, central Queensland, over four breeding seasons during 2006–09 inclusive, by observation and collection of orts and pellets from beneath nests. The Eagles raised six fledglings in nine pair-years (0.67 young/pair/year), including one brood of two fledglings, although siblicide was implicated at another nest. During each breeding season, eggs were laid in August; incubation lasted 37 ± 3 days, and the nestling period lasted 53–54 days to the ‘branching’ stage, at one nest. The breeding diet was 80% lizards and 20% small birds by number (*n* = 10) at two nests in one year. Some behavioural observations are presented.
