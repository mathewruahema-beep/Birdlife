### 

Bird Species in a Victorian Coastal Garden Spanning Eighteen Years After the 'Ash Wednesday' Bushfire on 16 February 1983

 Pauline Reilly
P. 77-84

### Abstract

Changes in the composition of bird species visiting an outlying garden of a small coastal village were monitored for 18 years following a severe bushfire that destroyed all but a few homes in the are in 1983. Without any surviving vegetation, bird species were few for the first four years. Records were sketchy then and are not included in this report. After 1987 a total of 82 species was recorded, with a range of 34 to 54 annually. Of the 82 species, 34 were resident (of which ten bred), 19 were regular seasonal visitors, eight were irregular visitors, and 21 were casual visitors.
