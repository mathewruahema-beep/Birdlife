### 

Predators of Nesting Little Terns *Sterna albifrons* at Forster, New South Wales

 A B Rose
P. 79-87

### Abstract

Information is presented on predation on nesting colonies of the endangered Little Tern *Sterna albifrons* on the mid-north coast of New South Wales. Predation by birds, mammals, crabs and ants is described.
