### 

Changes in Nankeen Kestrel *Falco cenchroides* breeding success at a nest-hollow after more than a decade

 Matthew Mo
pp. 137-140

### Abstract

Observations of Nankeen Kestrels *Falco cenchroides* using a nest-hollow on agricultural land south-west of Sydney, New South Wales, over four breeding seasons (1998–2001) were previously reported by Starr *et al*. (2004a,b). In the present study, the same nest-hollow was monitored over three breeding seasons (2014–2016) more than 10 years later, when it was used each year, but only one chick was raised each year. This represents a notable reduction in breeding success from the 3–4 chicks that were raised each year in 1998–2001.
