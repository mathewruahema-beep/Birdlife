### 

Notes on the Regent Honeyeater *Xanthomyza phrygia*

 Andrew Ley
P. 171-173

### Abstract

Based on observations on several breeding pairs of Regent Honeyeaters *Xanthomyza phrygia *information is presented on the colour of the facial patch, sexual dimorphism, nesting and mating behaviour, timing of nesting, aggression and calls.
