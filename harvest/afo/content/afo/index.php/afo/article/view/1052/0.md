### 

Age-related Colour Changes and Behaviour in the Northern Funereal Black-Cockatoo *Calyptorhynchus funereus funereus*

 John Courtney
pp. 137-145

### Abstract

The development of colour changes and behaviour from chick to adult in two captive males of the northern race of the Funereal Black-Cockatoo *Calyptorhynchus funereus **funereus *is described for the first time. These males had female-type characters (pale bill, dark orbital skin) until I year 10 months, but differed in having duller ear coverts. The upper mandible was dark by 2 years of age, and the orbital skin was pale within 3 years of fledging. Adult plumage (brown instead of black) was attained in two years and a dark lower mandible was attained at 4 years. Courtship displays were first performed at 22 1/2 months. There were slight differences in the quality, length and rate of utterance of juvenile begging and food-swallowing vocalisations when compared with the western race *C.f latirostris. *It is concluded *that funereus *may be specifically distinct from southern C. *f xanthonotus *and western *latirostris.*
