### 

The Letter-winged Kite Irruption of 1976-77

 David L G Hollands
pp. 1-18

### Abstract

In late 1976 and the first half of 1977 there was a movement of Letterwinged Kites *Elanus scriptus *out of the inland of Australia in numbers that far exceeded anything ever previously recorded. This paper is an attempt to summarise what is known of this irruption.
