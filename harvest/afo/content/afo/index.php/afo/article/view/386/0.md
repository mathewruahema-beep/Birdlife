### 

Forum—Do Tasmanian Southern Boobooks migrate?

 Jerry Olsen, S J.S Debus
p. 106-108

### Abstract

Tasmanian Southern Boobooks *Ninox novaeseelandiae leucopsis* are smaller, darker and more spotted than those on the Australian mainland. The occasional presence on the mainland of individuals with leucopsis-like features raises the question of whether these have migrated or dispersed to the mainland.
