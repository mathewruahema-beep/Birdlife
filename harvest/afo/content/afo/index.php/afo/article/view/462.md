### 

Multiple Matings by a Pair of Rainbow Lorikeets

 VALERIE LA MAY
p. 31-32

### Abstract

This note describes an observation of a pair of wild Rainbow Lorikeets *Trichoglossus haematodus* engaging in five successive copulations. Multiple matings in the wild have not been described in the literature for this species.
