### 

The Vertebrate Fauna of Point Nepean, Victoria II. Birds

 G F B Horrocks, G W Brown
P. 24-34

### Abstract

This paper details observations of the avifauna recorded on a series of field trips to the Point Nepean peninsula. Data were recorded intermittently for just over one year via census counting and individual sight records. A total of 148 bird species has been recorded from Point Nepean, of which 69 were recorded during the course of this study.
