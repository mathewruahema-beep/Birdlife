### 

Agonistic behaviour of the Red Wattlebird *Anthochaera carunculata* towards an elapid snake

 Matthew Mo, Scott Beno, Bel Manria

### Abstract

This note describes a Red Wattlebird *Anthochaera carunculata* directing a series of swoops at an Eastern Brown
 Snake Pseudonaja textilis. The mobbing interaction occurred while the snake was on the ground. Previous literature suggests that the Eastern Brown Snake could constitute a potential nest predator.
