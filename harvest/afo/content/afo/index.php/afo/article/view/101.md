### 

Observations of Australian Bitterns Eating Small Mammals

 Peter Menkhorst, Andrew Silcocks
P. 37-39

### Abstract

On two occasions (27 July and 3 August 2003) an Australasian Bittern *Botaurus poiciloptilus* was observed eating a small mammal, probably a House Mouse *Mus domesticus*, near Werribee, 35 km south-west of Melbourne, Victoria. These observations augment the little material published on foraging and diet of the species.
