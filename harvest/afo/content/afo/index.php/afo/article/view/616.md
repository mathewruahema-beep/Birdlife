### 

A Pacific Black Duck *Anas superciliosa* Eating Carrion

 P -J GUAY, J GREGURKE
p. 80-81

### Abstract

The Pacific Black Duck *Anas superciliosa* is a mostly herbivorous Australian dabbling duck. Here we present the first record of this species eating carrion, and we review literature on its diet.
