### 

A Sighting of a Green Sandpiper *Tringa ochropus* at Darwin, Northern Territory

 Niven McCrie
P. 229-232

### Abstract

This note documents a sighting of a Green sandpiper *Tringa ochropus* at Darwin, Northern Territory, on 4 March 1998. The record has been accepted by the Birds Australia Rarities Committee (Case 239, Tony Palliser in litt.), and is the first confirmed record in Australia.
