### 

Pink-breasted Females in a Population of Red-capped Robins *Petroica goodenovii*

 J N Hobbs
pp. 150-152

### Abstract

Three breeding female Red-capped Robins *Petroica goodenovii, *each having a pink wash on the breast, were seen at Dareton, New South Wales in 1984. Absence of information on this plumage in current field guides and reference books is outlined. The ratio of such females, coloured males and brown males in a breeding population at Dareton is given; 15% of the breeding females had pink on the breast
