### 

Nankeen Kestrel *Falco cenchroides* Takes Crested Pigeon *Ocyphaps lophotes*

 Damon L Oliver
P. 47-48

### Abstract

This note details a Crested Pigeon *Ocyphaps lophotes* as a prey item of a Nankeen kestrel *Falco cenchroides*. The Kestrel apparently killed the Pigeon, of perhaps similar weight to the Kestrel, and was able to carry it with difficulty into a strong head wind.
