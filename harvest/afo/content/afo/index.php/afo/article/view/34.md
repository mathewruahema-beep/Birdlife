### 

Notes on the Behaviour of the Rufus Bristlebird *Dasyornis broadbenti*

 John M Peter
P. 147-151

### Abstract

Most behavioural aspects of the Rufus Bristlebird *Dasyornis broadbenti*, apart from its song, are poorly known. As a result of opportunistic observations between 1998 and 2000 near Aireys Inlet and Anglesea, Victoria, three different types of behaviour (courtship feeding, interspecific relations and amplification of song) of the vulnerable subspecies *D. b. caryochrous* of southern Victoria are described.
