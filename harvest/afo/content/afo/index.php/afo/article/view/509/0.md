### 

Diet of the Nankeen Kestrel *Falco cenchroides* on Christmas Island

 MARTIN SCHULZ, LINDY LUMSDEN
p. 28-32

### Abstract

This note reports on the diet of the Nankeen Kestrel *Falco cenchroides* on Christmas Island (Indian Ocean) from remains located at perches in 2005 and 2008. The dominant prey item was the Giant Grasshopper* Valanga irregularis*. Other prey included the Christmas Island Swiftlet *Collocalia linchi natalis* and the introduced Grass Skink* Lygosoma bowringii*.
