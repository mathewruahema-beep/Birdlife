A Grey-headed Honeyeater with an Extra Hallux

 PERRY de REBEIRA, ALMA de REBEIRA

### Abstract

This note describes a Grey-headed Honeyeater *Lichenostomus keartlandi* with an extra hallux on the tarsus of its right leg.
