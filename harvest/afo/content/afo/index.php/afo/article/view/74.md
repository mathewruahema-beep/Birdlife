### 

Courtship behaviour of the Barking Owl

 William Riddell
pp. 203-205

### Abstract

This note describes allopreening, of the head but also the legs, in a breeding pair of Barking Owls *Ninox connivens* in the Darwin Botanic Gardens, Darwin, NT. A fledgling also allopreened its parent.
