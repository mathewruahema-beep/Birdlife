### 

Birds of Narran Lake Nature Reserve, New South Wales

 Andrew J Ley
P. 38-54

### Abstract

The 181 species of birds recorded from the Narran Lake Nature Reserve are listed. A further 19 species are on a supplementary list of doubtful records from the Nature Reserve and of potential additions to the full list. Occurrence and breeding activity of waterbirds in the Nature Reserve in 1998-2001 inclusive are documented. The Nature Reserve experienced high floods in October 1998 and April 1999; a large breeding event took place on the first of these but little breeding occurred on the second.
