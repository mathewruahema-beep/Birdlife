### 

New Insights into Spangled Drongo Migration Across Torres Strait

 MICHAEL TARBURTON
p. 25-27

### Abstract

Ten flocks of Spangled Drongos* Dicrurus bracteatus* observed in their southward migration across Torres Strait in November 2008 were farther east than most records, and flock-sizes were outside the given range of two authorities.
