### 

The Status and Breeding of *Artamus *spp. on King Island

 M T Templeton, A M McGarvie
pp. 101-102

### Abstract

Four *Artamus *species have been recorded for King Island over the past ten years (Green & McGarvie, 1971, *Birds of King Island) . *In 1972 *A. superciliosus *and *A. personatus *were found breeding for the first time, while *A . cyanopterus *was found breeding for the first time in the past 16 years, although it was reported by old residents to have been a regular breeding species at Pearshape in the past.
