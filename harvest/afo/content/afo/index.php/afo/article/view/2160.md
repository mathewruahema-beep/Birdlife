### 

Nest, egg, incubation behaviour and vocalisations of the New Guinea endemic Black Pitohui *Melanorectes nigrescens*

 Richard H. Donaghey, Donna J. Belder, Tony Baylis
pp. 116-120

### Abstract

The Black Pitohui *Melanorectes nigrescens* (Pachycephalidae) is endemic to mid-mountain forests in mainland New Guinea. Spectrograms of three different songs recorded in the Yopno Urawa Som Conservation Area (YUS CA), Huon Peninsula, Papua New Guinea, are presented. We describe the elevation and nest-site, height above the ground, structure and materials of a nest with an egg, discovered in the YUS CA. The colour and dimensions of the egg are documented. We present photographs of the nest-site, nest, and egg, and compare these with previously described eggs and a nest. We observed only the female Black Pitohui incubating the single-egg clutch. We compare the meagre information on pitohui breeding biology with that of Australian whistlers and shrike-thrushes.
