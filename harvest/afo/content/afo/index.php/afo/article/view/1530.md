Notes on the Gull-Billed Tern

 P A Bourke, V T Lowe, T G Lowe

### Abstract

This paper is in two parts, the first dealing with references to the species in the literature, and the second covering observations at a nesting colony at Lake Tutchewop, near Mystic Park, Victoria.
