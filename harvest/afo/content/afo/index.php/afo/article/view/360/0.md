### 

Christmas Island Goshawk predation on a juvenile tropicbird *Phaethon *sp.

 Valerie La May
pp. 50-52

### Abstract

This note describes an adult Christmas Island Goshawk *Accipiter hiogaster natalis* feeding on a juvenile tropicbird* Phaethon* sp. This observation provides further insight into the little-known foraging ecology and behaviour of the Christmas Island Goshawk, and augments previous records of predation by this goshawk on seabirds and their chicks.
