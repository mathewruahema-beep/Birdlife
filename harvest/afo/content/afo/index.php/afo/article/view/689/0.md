### 

The Blue Bird of Paradise *Paradisaea rudolphi: *Display and Behaviour of Wild Birds

 Richard Whiteside
P. 319-327

### Abstract

Regular field observations were conducted on Blue Birds of Paradise *Paradisaea rudolphi *at Kompiam, Enga Province, Papua New Guinea, from February 1995 to January 1996. Adult male display behaviour and vocalisations are described. These birds possibly interacted with male Carola's Parotias *Parotia carolae *and Magnificent Birds of Paradise *Diphyllodes magnificus. *Male Blue Birds of Paradise were fully plumed and displayed from April to late November/early December; they moulted over 3-5 months from November/December to March/ April. Males foraged solitarily at all levels in the forest for fruits; female-plumaged birds often foraged with other birds of paradise. Advanced nestlings and fledglings were recorded in December; juvenile plumage is described. A large nestling was fed on fruits, insects and a skink.
