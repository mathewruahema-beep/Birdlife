### 

Description of a Swamp Harrier *Circus approximans* Nest in Buttongrass Moorland

 MICHAEL DRIESSEN
p. 47-48

### Abstract

This note describes a Swamp Harrier* Circus approximans* nest found in Buttongrass Moorland in western Tasmania. Although Swamp Harrier nests are known from vegetation containing sedges and rushes, this is the first description of a nest from this vegetation type.
