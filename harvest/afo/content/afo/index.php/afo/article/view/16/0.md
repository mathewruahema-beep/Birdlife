### 

A recent specimen of a Long-tailed Cuckoo from Lord Howe Island

 Walter E Boles, Leah Tsang, Jaynia Sladek
pp. 53-55

### Abstract

An adult female Long-tailed Cuckoo *Urodynamis taitensis* (also known as *Eudynamys taitensis*), killed by hitting a window on Lord Howe Island on 2 December 2010, constitutes the the sixth record and only the third specimen from the Island and the only individual for which the sex is known.
