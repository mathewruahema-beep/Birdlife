### 

Unusual Nests of the Laughing Kookaburra *Dacelo novaeguineae*

 L H Smith
P. 197-199

### Abstract

An account is given of two unusual nests of the Laughing Kookaburra *Dacelo novaeguineae, *one in the bank of a creek and the other composed of sticks in a clump of mistletoe in a Yellow Gum *Eucalyptus leucoxylon*, in Tarnagulla, Victoria.
