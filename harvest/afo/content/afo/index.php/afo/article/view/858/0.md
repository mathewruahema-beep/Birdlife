### 

Notes on the Birds of the Upper and Middle Reaches of Kimberley Rivers during the Dry Season, 1989

 T Aumann
P. 51-67

### Abstract

Distributional data are given for 171 avian species occurring on or near rivers of the Kimberley region of Western Australia during the dry season (April-November) of 1989. Additional data are also supplied for some of these species, particularly for raptors.
