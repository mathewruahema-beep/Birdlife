### 

Rainbow Lorikeets apparently harvesting orange fungal spores from the underside of willow leaves

 Hugh A. Ford
p. 29

### Abstract

The diet of Rainbow Lorikeets **Trichoglossus moluccanu*s* consists largely of nectar and pollen, with some fruit, leaf buds, bark and insects and, more recently, meat provided at bird-feeders. Here, I describe an instance of Rainbow Lorikeets apparently harvesting fungal spores from the underside of leaves of Weeping Willow* Salix babylonica*, a food item not previously recorded.
