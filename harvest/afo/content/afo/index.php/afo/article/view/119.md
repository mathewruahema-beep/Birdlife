### 

Jouanin's Petrel *Bulweria fallax* in Australia

 Mike Carter
P. 69-76

### Abstract

A record of two Jouanin's Petrels *Bulweria fallax* seen over continental slope waters south-west of Ashmore Reef on 15 October 2000 has been accepted by the Birds Australia Rarities Committee. This paper documents the occurrence, which is a first record for Australia, refers to other potential sightings and discusses identification criteria.
