### 

A Pale ‘Cream’ Satin Bowerbird *Ptilonorhynchus violaceus* (Family Ptilonorhynchidae): First Documented Evidence of Aberrant Plumage in any Bowerbird

 Clifford B Frith, Terry Murphy
p. 40-44

### Abstract

An adult female or female-plumaged immature male wild Satin Bowerbird* Ptilonorhynchus violaceus* in a predominantly pale ‘cream’ plumage was observed and photographed in a Kendall, New South Wales, garden over a 6-week period. This individual represents not only the first fully documented instance recorded in the literature of aberrant plumage in the Satin Bowerbird but also in the entire bowerbird family. Colour photographic evidence of the aberrant individual Satin Bowerbird is presented and discussed.
