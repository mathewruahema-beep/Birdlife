### 

Diet of an Eastern Grass Owl at Kamay Botany Bay National Park, Sydney

 MARTIN SCHULZ, ELIZABETH MAGAREY
p. 177-178

### Abstract

A single Eastern Grass Owl *Tyto longimembris* was observed on the Kurnell Peninsula, Botany Bay (Sydney, NSW) in July 2009 and February 2010. Pellets collected from its roosts on the latter occasion (*n* = 8 pellets), and in April 2010 (*n* = 1 pellet), contained the remains of introduced rodents (Black Rat *Rattus rattus*, 89% of pellets; House Mouse *Mus musculus*, 22%) and one Brown Quail *Coturnix ypsilophora*.
