### 

Wild-caught Long-billed Corella *Cacatua tenuirostris *x Sulphur-crested Cockatoo C. *galerita *hybrids from Western Australia

 Barry Kentish, David Brennan
P. 76-78

### Abstract

Three birds trapped in western Victoria in 2002 as part of the cockatoo-management programppeared to be Long-billed Corella *Cacatua tenuirostris *X Sulphur-crested Cockatoo C. *galerita *hybrids. This evidence suggests that interbreeding between these species occurs in the wild.
