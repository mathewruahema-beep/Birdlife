### 

Wood-Swallows in Mid-Northern Victoria

 V T Lowe, T G Lowe
pp. 205-210

### Abstract

The seemingly contradictory movements of some species of Wood-Swallows and their unpredictable selection of seasonal breeding grounds is discussed. It is shown that their annual migratory arrival and departure dates for Victoria can be predicted to within a few days. Evidence is given of large scale nectar-feeding, and *Artamus cinereus* is shown to be breeding in Victoria.
