### 

Behaviour of probable hybrid Red-backed x Superb Fairy-wrens at Gladstone, Queensland

 Martin Ross, Allan Briggs

### Abstract

This note describes observations of the behaviour of two hybrid male fairy-wrens (most likely resulting from crosses between Red-backed *Malurus melanocephalus* and Superb Fairy-wrens *M. cyaneus*) at Gladstone, Queensland, and their pairing with two female Red-backed Fairy-wrens.
