### 

Peregrine Falcon *Falco peregrinus* of the Siberian Subspecies *calidus* on Christmas Island

 MIKE CARTER, ANDREW SILCOCKS
p. 174-176

### Abstract

This note records the occurrence of a Siberian Peregrine Falcon *Falco peregrinus calidus* on Christmas Island, Indian Ocean, in January 2010: apparently the second record for Australian territory of this migratory, Arctic subspecies. This is also the fifth record of a vagrant Peregrine Falcon on Christmas Island, although the others could not be identified to subspecies.
