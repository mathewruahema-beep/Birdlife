### 

Second Mainland Australian Record of the Oriental Honey-Buzzard *Pernis ptilorhyncus*

 GREGORY PHIL
p. 90-92

### Abstract

This note documents the second occurrence of an Oriental Honey-Buzzard *Pernis ptilorhyncus* for mainland Australia, in the Top End of the Northern Territory.
