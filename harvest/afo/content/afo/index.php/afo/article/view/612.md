### 

Aggressive Behaviour by Australian Owlet-nightjars Defending Fledglings

 LISA I DOUCETTE
P. 60-63

### Abstract

I report observations of aggressive behaviour by Australian Owlet-nightjars *Aegotheles cristatus* defending newly fledged juveniles. Aggressive behaviour in Owlet-nightjars is uncommon, and an ‘attack’ on another species has not been described previously.
