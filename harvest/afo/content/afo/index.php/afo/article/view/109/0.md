### 

Pechora Pipit *Anthus gustavi* in Australia

 Mike Carter
P. 59-65

### Abstract

A Pechora Pipit *Anthus gustavi* was present on West Island, Ashmore Reef, off the northern coast of Western Australia on 4 and 5 November 2001. This paper documents the occurrence, the first record for Australia.
