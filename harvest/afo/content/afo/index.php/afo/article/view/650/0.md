### 

Shining Bronze-Cuckoo Associating with and Imitating Alarm Call of Chestnut-rumped Heathwren

 DANNY I ROGERS, KEN G ROGERS, JOAN SANDBRINK
p 42-45

### Abstract

We describe an observation of a juvenile Shining Bronze-Cuckoo *Chrysococcyx lucidus* being attended by a pair of Chestnut-rumped Heathwrens *Hylacola pyrrhopygia*, apparently the first record of this cuckoo–host association. An alarm call given by the young Cuckoo appeared identical to that of the female Heathwren, and we suggest that this was a case of vocal mimicry.
