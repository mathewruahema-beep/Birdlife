### 

Yellow-Plumed Honeyeaters *Lichenostomos ornatus* Milking the Fruit of Ruby Saltbush Enchylaena tomentosa

 M Brooker, L Brooker
P. 81-82

### Abstract

This note details the unusual foraging behaviour of the Yellow-Plumed Honeyeater *Lichenostomus ornatus.* The honeyeaters were apparently milking the fruits of Ruby Saltbush *Enchylaena tomentose.*
