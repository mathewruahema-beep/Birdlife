### 

The Importance of Remnant Vegetation to the Blue Bonnet in North-western Victoria

 W B Emison
P. 159-164

### Abstract

Bird censuses throughout the Mallee Region (1984-88) revealed that the Blue Bonnet *Nonhiella haematogaster* occurs mainly in remnants of mallee in the otherwise cleared agricultural land of northwestern Victoria. The long-term ability of these mallee remnants to support the Blue Bonnet is uncertain. Concern is also expressed about possible competition for nesting hollows between the introduced Common Starling *Stumus vulgaris *and the Blue Bonnet.
