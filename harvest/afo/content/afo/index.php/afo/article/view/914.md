### 

A Bulwer's Petrel *Bulweria bulwerii *in Victoria

 Mike Carter, Tim Reid

### Abstract

A Victorian record of Bulwer's Petrel *Bulweria bulwerii *is documented and discussed in relation to previous reports in the Australian region and vagrancy in the Atlantic.
