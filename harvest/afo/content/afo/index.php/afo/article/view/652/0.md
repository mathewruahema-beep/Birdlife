### 

Do Grey Currawongs Eat Carrion?

 GRAHAM R FULTON
p. 48-49

### Abstract

Fourteen days of observations of carrion feeding from a dead Common Brushtail Possum *Trichosurus vulpecula* carcass are reported. The Grey Currawong *Strepera versicolor*, Australian Magpie *Gymnorhina tibicen* and Australian Raven *Corvus coronoides* were present on each of the 14 days, yet only the Australian Raven was observed to feed from the carcass.
