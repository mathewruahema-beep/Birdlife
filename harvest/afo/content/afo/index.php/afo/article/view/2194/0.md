### 

On the dark morph of the Pygmy Eagle *Hieraaetus weiskei*

 S J.S. Debus
pp. 129-131

### Abstract

The juvenile dark morph of the Pygmy Eagle *Hieraaetus weiskei* remains essentially undescribed, as here I reidentify a published photograph of one such alleged individual cited in an early paper on the morphs of the allospecific Little Eagle* H. morphnoides*. The problem photograph illustrates the potential for confusion between the dark-morph Pygmy Eagle
(or indeed Little Eagle) and the juvenile Brahminy Kite *Haliastur indus* (Australasian subspecies *H. i. girrenera*).
