### 

The breeding biology of the Topknot Pigeon *Lopholaimus antarcticus* in south-eastern Queensland

 Phillip A. Robson
pp. 141-147

### Abstract

Breeding activity of two pairs of Topknot Pigeons *Lopholaimus antarcticus* in south-eastern Queensland was observed for 47.5 hours over 15 days in January–February 2019. Pair 1 was observed commencing early in the incubation stage and continued until the squab (nestling) was found dead at age 13 days. Change-over behaviour and routine are described, as are responses by the brooding birds to potential bird predators and human-generated noise. No vocalisations by the adults were detected at or near the nest. The brooding effort was equally shared between the male and female, with the male brooding during the day and the female at night. Squab growth and development are described with the aid of
photographs. Pair 2 was observed procuring nesting material.
