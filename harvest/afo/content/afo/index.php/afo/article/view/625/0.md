### 

A King Penguin *Aptenodytes patagonicus* at Port MacDonnell, South Australia

 ROBERT F FARNES, PETER S LANSLEY, ROBERT GREEN
p. 180-183

### Abstract

A King Penguin *Aptenodytes patagonicus*, ashore to moult, was present at Port MacDonnell, South Australia, for at least 13 days from 17 to 29 February 2004. This record, Birds Australia Rarities Committee Case no. 424, represents the second for South Australia, fifth for the Australian mainland, and, by including the concentration of Tasmanian records, approximately the 30th overall. A description is presented from field notes and photographs. The bird died shortly after being taken from the beach, and a brief post-mortem report is included.
