### 

Southern Boobook *Ninox novaeseelandiae* Feeding on the Fungus *Pislithus albus* in North Queensland

 EUAN G RITCHIE, JENNY K MARTIN
p. 46-47

### Abstract

This note describes an observation of a Southern Boobook *Ninox novaeseelandiae* pecking at the fruiting body of a terrestrial fungus in northern Queensland.
