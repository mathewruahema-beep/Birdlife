### 

Spotted Redshank *Tringa erythropus *in Australia

 Mike Carter, Aidan Sudbury
P. 149-159

### Abstract

There are six reports of Spotted Redshank *Tringa erythropus *having occurred in Australia, two of which are now properly validated records. One of these is of a bird present near Melbourne in the autumn of 1992. This occurrence is documented and previous reports are noted and discussed.
