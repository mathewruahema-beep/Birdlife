### 

A Red-capped Flowerpecker* Dicaeum (pectorale) geelvinkianum* on Saibai Island, Torres Strait, Australia

 Peter S Lansley
P. 33-36

### Abstract

Details are presented of the first sighting in Australia of a Red-capped or Papuan Flowerpecker *Dicaeum (pectoral) geelvinkianum* on Saibai Island in Torres Strait, Queensland. This record has been accepted by the Birds Australia Rarities Committee (BARC case number 273).
