### 

Birds of a Railway Yard at Corinda, Queensland

 Ian N Walters
pp. 17-27

### Abstract

Birds encountered at an industrial-residential site were examined to see if patterns of species abundance would provide an indication of environmental disturbance. Observations from 1972 to 1979 revealed that the site was impoverished in native species. Feral species dominated. It is concluded that such small-area studies may be useful in the context of urban planning.
