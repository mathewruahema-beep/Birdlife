### 

Seasonal Changes in Bill Colour in Female Rufus Whistlers

 Jo Wieneke
P. 28-30

### Abstract

The results of field observations, and an examination of museum specimens, are presented showing that female Rufus Whistlers *Pachycephala rufiventris* have pale bills in winter and black bills when breeding (in summer).
