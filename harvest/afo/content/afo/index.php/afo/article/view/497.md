### 

Silver Gulls *Chroicocephalus novaehollandiae* Harassing Foraging Yellow-billed Spoonbills *Platalea flavipes*

 MICHAEL CONNOR
p. 184-185

### Abstract

Silver Gulls *Chroicocephalus novaehollandiae* harassed foraging Yellow-billed Spoonbills *Platalea flavipes* to gain access to their feeding locations. The interaction between the species appears to be intermediate between strict commensalism and true kleptoparasitism.
