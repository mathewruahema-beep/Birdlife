### 

Roosting Habits of the Red-whiskered Bulbul at Wollongong, New South Wales

 K A Wood
P. 75-80

### Abstract

Roosting habits of the Red-whiskered Bulbul *Pycnonotus jocosus *were observed from 1982 and recorded in detail from 1993 to 1996. The communal roost was a patch of dense Lantana *Lantana camara *(c. 0.5 ha) in the centre of a public reserve. From 1993 to 1996, the number of roosting birds was highest in winter months (mean = 153, range 90-240, n = 6) and lowest in summer months (mean= 31, range 7-75, n = 4). Roosting behaviour is described.
