### 

Chinese Pond-Heron *Ardeola bacchus* in the Cocos (Keeling) Islands: A New Bird for Australian Territory

 MIKE CARTER, RICHARD BAXTER
p. 54-59

### Abstract

A Chinese Pond-Heron *Ardeola bacchus*, thought to be a first-summer female, was present on West Island, Cocos (Keeling) Islands, from 3 to 7 May 2006. This is an account of the occurrence, the first confirmed record of the species for Australian territory.
