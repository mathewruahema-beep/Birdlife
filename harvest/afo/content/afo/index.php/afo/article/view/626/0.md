### 

Diet and Roost-site Fidelity in the Common Koel *Eudynamys scolopacea* in Suburban Darwin

 CHRIS HEALEY, EMMELINE HEALEY
p. 184-186

### Abstract

The Common Koel *Eudynamys scolopacea* is a common breeding migrant to the Top End of the Northern Territory. This paper provides information on previously unrecorded foods, and reports observations of an adult male that occupied an unusual daytime roosting and feeding tree for eight consecutive days.
