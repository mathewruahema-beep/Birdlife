### 

Fairy and Little Terns Breeding on Tasmania's East Coast

 J R Napier
P. 103-109

### Abstract

General observations on the breeding of the Fairy Tern, *Sterna nereis*, on Tasmania's east coast are related, together with a more detailed account of the breeding of the Little Tern.* S. albifrons*, in the same area. Brief notes are given of the differences between the two species, discernible in the field with the aid of binoculars.
