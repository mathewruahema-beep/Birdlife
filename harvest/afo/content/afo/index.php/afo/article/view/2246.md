### 

A Crow Honeyeater *Gymnomyza aubryana* escapes a Black Rat *Rattus rattus* attack at its nest with its egg

 Pascal Villard, Jean-Marc Mériot, Pierre Boudinot

### Abstract

We report on recordings made with an infrared video of a Black Rat *Rattus rattus* attacking a female Crow Honeyeater *Gymnomyza aubryana* and egg on her nest at night, and subsequent escape of the Honeyeater with the egg carried in her beak.
