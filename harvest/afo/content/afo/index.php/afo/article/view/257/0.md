### 

Diet of the Black-shouldered Kite *Elanus axillaris* in New South Wales

 Deryk Engel, A B Rose
P. 211-213

### Abstract

The breeding diet of a pair of Black-shouldered Kites *Elanus axillaris* and a single nestling was studied in outer Sydney, N.S.W., over spring-summer 1995-96, by pellet analysis (n=59). House Mouse *Mus domesticus* occurred in 58 pellets (98%), Black Rat *Rattus rattus* in one (2%), and skink scales in five (8%). House Mice also predominated in pellets at an inland site (all of 26 pellets) and two coastal sites (eight of 10 pellets, all of four pellets) in New South Wales, and in stomachs of Kites found dead at four sites in New South Wales (mice in three of four Kites). Other dietary items were native rodents and skinks.
