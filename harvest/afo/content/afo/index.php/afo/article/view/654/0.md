### 

Long-tailed, Not Arctic, Jaeger at Jervis Bay in 1976: First Record for the Australian Capital Territory (Coastal Section)

 CHRIS SONTER
p. 55-56

### Abstract

An alleged specimen of the Arctic Jaeger *Stercorarius parasiticus* from Jervis Bay, Australian Capital Territory, in March 1976 is here re-identified as a Long-tailed Jaeger *S. longicaudus*, and becomes the first record of the latter species for the ACT.
