### 

Distraction Display of the Hoary-headed Grebe

 JOHN M PETER
p. 42-43

### Abstract

Two different distraction displays of a nesting Hoary-headed Grebe *Poliocephalus poliocephalus* are described. These are compared with previous descriptions of crash-diving for this species and for the New Zealand Dabchick *P. rufopectus*, and with the silent slipping away from the nest by Australasian Grebes *Tachybaptus novaehollandiae*.
