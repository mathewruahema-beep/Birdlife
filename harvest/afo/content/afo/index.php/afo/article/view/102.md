### 

Brown Goshawk *Accipiter fasciatus* Attacking a Striated Heron *Butorides striatus* over the Sea

 Clive Minton
P. 45-46

### Abstract

This note describes an unsuccessful attack by a Brown Goshawk *Accipiter fasciatus* on a Striated Heron *Butorides striatus* that was flying over the sea.
