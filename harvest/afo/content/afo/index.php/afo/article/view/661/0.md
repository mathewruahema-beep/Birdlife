### 

Nominate Subspecies of the Little Egret *Egretta garzetta garzetta* in Australian Territory

 MIKE CARTER, PETER MENKHORST
p. 104-108

### Abstract

In 2005 Little Egrets of the nominate subspecies *Egretta garzetta garzetta* were observed at Darwin, Northern Territory, and at Werribee, near Melbourne, Victoria. These occurrences, which we believe are the first for mainland Australia, are here documented.
