### 

First Breeding Record of the Spotted Whistling-Duck* Dendrocygna guttora* in Australia

 Sue Gould, Michael Barnett
P. 196-200

### Abstract

The observations and photographs presented here provide evidence of the Spotted Whistling-Duck *Dendrocygna guttora* nesting in Australia. This occurrence extends the global breeding distribution of the species.
