### 

A snapshot in the post-fledging period of the Black Falcon

 C P Barnes, S J.S Debus
p. 86-88

### Abstract

This note describes a prolonged aerial interaction between three Black Falcons *Falco subniger*, consisting of two juveniles in their post-fledging dependence period and an adult male, presumably their parent. This incident, along with previous fragmentary observations, supports the view that the Black Falcon’s post-fledging period will prove to be similar to that of other large, bird-eating falcons in Australia and elsewhere.
