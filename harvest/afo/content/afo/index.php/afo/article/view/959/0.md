### 

Little Bitterns Breeding in Northern Australia

 Roger P Jaensch
P. 217-221

### Abstract

The Little Bittern *Ixobrychus minutus *was first recorded in the north of Western Australia in January 1982 and March 1983, in extensive tall rushland of *Typha *at Packsaddle Swamp, near Kununurra. Three nests with eggs of the Little Bittern were found there in January 1985; this was the first record of breeding by this species in northern Australia. The occurrence of Little Bitterns near Kununurra may be a result of migration from southern Australia.
