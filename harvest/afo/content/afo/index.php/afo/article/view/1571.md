### 

The Birds of "Green Mountains"

 W R Wheeler
pp. 257-269

### Abstract

An account is given of the birds that were observed by the members of the Bird Observers Club, during three visits to O'Reilly's " Green Mountains"; two groups in 1968 and one in 1970. References are also given to the birds of the Lamington National Park, "Mundoolun" Station near Canungra, and other parts of the district.
