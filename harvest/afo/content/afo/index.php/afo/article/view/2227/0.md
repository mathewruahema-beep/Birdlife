### 

Eurasian Hobby *Falco subbuteo* : A review of recent records from Western Australia and Australian external territories

 Marcus J.C. Singor

### Abstract

All Australian records of the Eurasian Hobby *Falco subbuteo* are summarised. Before 2016, the only Australian records of the Eurasian Hobby were on external territories to the north-west of the mainland. Each year between 2016 and 2021 a single Eurasian Hobby was observed as an austral summer visitor to the Swan Coastal Plain in south-western Western Australia. As it appears that multiple individuals have been recorded across years, it is likely that the species is now more than just an accidental vagrant to Australia.
