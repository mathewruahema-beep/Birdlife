### 

Observations of Commensal Foraging Associations Between the Common Blackbird and Native Australian Species

 JOHN M PETER
p. 207-208

### Abstract

This note describes two cases of a native bird species feeding commensally with the introduced Common Blackbird *Turdus merula* while the latter was feeding on the ground: juvenile Masked Lapwings *Vanellus miles*, and a Brown Thornbill *Acanthiza pusilla*. In both cases the native species followed, and appeared to benefit from, the Blackbird’s foraging activities.
