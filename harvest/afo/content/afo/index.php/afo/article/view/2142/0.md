### 

Diet of the Peregrine Falcon *Falco peregrinus* in inland south-western Australia

 Russell Palmer, A. B. Rose, S. J.S. Debus
pp. 36-39

### Abstract

The diet of the Peregrine Falcon *Falco peregrinus* in inland south-western Australia was investigated from a sample of 73 pellets collected in August–November 2006, from cliff nest-sites and nearby roosts in the banded iron formation ranges of the Goldfields around Koolyanobbing, Western Australia. The diet was 94% birds (*n* = 80; about half being Purple-crowned Lorikeets *Glossopsitta porphyrocephala*) and 6% mammals (microbats, rodent) by number, and 98% birds (59% parrots) and 2% mammals by biomass. The prey profile reflects the bird assemblages in the extensive, intact Great Western Woodlands rather than that in rural environments of south-eastern Australia where other dietary studies have been conducted.
