### 

The Rediscovery of the Mallee Whipbird in Victoria

 T J Hunt, R F Kenyon
P. 222-226

### Abstract

An account is given of the rediscovery of the Mallee or Western Whipbird in Victoria. There is a description of the habitat and an account of other species wlith which the Mallee Whipbird was found to be associated.
