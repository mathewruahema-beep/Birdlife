### 

Twelve Years of Winter Counts of the Orange-bellied Parrot *Neophema chrysogaster*

 Jonathan Starks, Peter Brown, Richard Loyn, Peter Menkhorst
P. 305-312

### Abstract

Orange-bellied Parrots *Neophema chrysogaster *were counted annually in late July from 1979 to 1990 in southern Victoria and south-eastern South Australia. The minimum total winter population averaged 83.9 birds (range 67-126), with no significant change in numbers over the 12 years. The main wintering areas were Point Wilson, Lake Connewarre and Swan Island in Victoria, and Carpenter Rocks in South Australia. The counts were made possible only by the dedication of teams of volunteers.
