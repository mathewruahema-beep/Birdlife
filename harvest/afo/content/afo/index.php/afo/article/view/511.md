### 

A Novel Food Item and Foraging Strategy for the White-faced Heron* Egretta novaehollandiae*

 MARK J ANTOS, GLENN K EHMKE, GRAINNE S MAGUIRE
p. 36-38

### Abstract

This note reports two opportunistic observations of a White-faced Heron* Egretta novaehollandiae* foraging on a road-killed Bennett’s Wallaby *Macropus rufogriseus* on King Island, Tasmania, in April 2008. Bennett’s Wallaby has not been recorded previously as a food item of this species, and records of opportunistic foraging on road-killed mammals by this heron are scant. Possible reasons for this novel foraging behaviour are explored.
