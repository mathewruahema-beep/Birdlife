### 

Some Breeding Records of the Common Koel *Eudynamys scolopacea*

 Marion M Crouther
pp. 49-56

### Abstract

Information is presented on Common Koe1s *Eudynamys scolopacea *breeding at Pleystowe, Queensland, and using Figbirds *Sphecotheres viridis *as hosts. Observations on Koels after fledging include descriptions of plumage changes in young birds prior to dispersaL Some comments are also included on information on the Common Koel in other references.
