### 

The Changing Status of the Rainbow Lorikeet *Trichoglossus haematodus* in South-east Australia: the Role of Wild and Escaped Birds

 Philip A Veerman
P. 3-9

### Abstract

Based on evidence from observations in Melbourne and Canberra, taken as sample points where many data are available, this note documents the increase in abundance of the Rainbow Lorikeet *Trichoglossus haematodus *in south-east Australia. I also present new data consistent with regular movements southwards during the warmer months and northwards in autumn.
