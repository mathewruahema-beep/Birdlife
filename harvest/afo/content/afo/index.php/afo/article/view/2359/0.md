### 

Intense sunning by an Eastern Whipbird *Psophodes olivaceus*

 Clifford B. Frith, Angus Emmott

### Abstract

An Eastern Whipbird *Psophodes olivaceus* was observed and photographed performing an intense ‘spreadeagle’ sunning posture as the first record of sunning by the species and genus. The function of sunning in birds is briefly reviewed and discussed.
