### 

Predation of Eastern Yellow Robin nestlings from the same nest by an Eastern Ringtail Possum and a Pied Currawong

 Nicholas Yu

### Abstract

This note describes the predation of nestling Eastern Yellow Robins *Eopsaltria australis* by an Eastern Ringtail Possum *Pseudocheirus peregrinus* and by a Pied Currawong *Strepera graculina* and likely attempted predation by a Red Wattlebird *Anthochaera carunculata*. Although Eastern Ringtail Possums have been inferred to be predators of birdsâ€™ nests, the observations and video presented here appear to be the first unequivocal evidence of carnivory in that species and offer new insights to this possum speciesâ€™ ecology.
