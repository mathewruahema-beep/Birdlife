### 

Little Ravens *Corvus mellori* Eating Feijoa *Acca sellowiana* Flowers

 Virgil Hubregtse
P. 146

### Abstract

Observations in a suburban garden in Melbourne, Victoria, add the Little Raven *Corvus mellori* to the list of Australian birds known to eat the exotic ornamental Feijoa *Acca sellowiana.*
