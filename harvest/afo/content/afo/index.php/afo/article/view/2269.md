### 

Orange pigment variety in Black-bellied Crimson Finch *Neochmia phaeton phaeton* from West Kimberley, Australia

 Naomi Blondel, Pippa Kern

### Abstract

During standardised bird surveys in September 2021 in the West Kimberly, two independent observations of an orange pigment variety in a male Black-bellied Crimson Finch *Neochmia phaeton phaeton* were recorded. This is the first documented observation of a wild Black-bellied Crimson Finch with this coloration.**
