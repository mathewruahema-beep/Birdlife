### 

Predation of lizards by the White-faced Heron *Egretta novaehollandiae*

 Matthew Mo
pp. 95-96

### Abstract

This note reports two observations of White-faced Herons *Egretta novaehollandiae* preying on lizards. Although predation of herpetofauna is well-known for Ardeidae species, predation on lizards by the White-faced Heron appears to have been recorded rarely, with just two published reports located.
