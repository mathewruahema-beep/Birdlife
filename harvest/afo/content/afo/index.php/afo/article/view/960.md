### 

The Status of Bird Species in the Rural Town of Harvey, Western Australia

 Eric H Sedgwick
P. 222-232

### Abstract

Bird species present in the town of Harvey (Western Australia) are treated in terms of patterns of usage and relative frequency (for 324 observation periods over 108 months, July 1977 to June 1986). Based on their frequency of occurrence, the 83 species recorded were allocated to one of four categories: residents (11 species); seasonal migrants and frequent visitors (19 species); occasional visitors (44 species); and visitors observed before the survey began (9 species). Most of the residents and some regular visitors breed on the town site (14 breeding species, 12 native).
