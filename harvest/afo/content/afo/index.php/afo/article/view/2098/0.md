### 

Piecing the puzzle together: the fate of the Night Parrot nest found in Western Australia by Jackett *et al*. (2017)

 Neil Hamilton, Allan Burbidge, Tegan Douglas, Lyle Gilbert
pp. 151-154

### Abstract

Characteristics of a Night Parrot nest in the East Murchison region of Western Australia are documented. This nest failed, most likely either due to predation or abandonment. Human disturbance is ruled out as a cause. However, we recommend that human activity near Night Parrot nests is minimised, to avoid unnecessary disturbance to breeding attempts in this Critically Endangered species.
