### 

Rainbow Lorikeets *Trichoglossus haematodus* Feeding on Psyllid Lerps

 Ian Endersby
P. 42-45

### Abstract

Rainbow Lorikeets *Trichoglossus haematodus* removed honeydew from the interior surface of lerps before discarding them. This feeding method differs from those honeyeaters which eat the whole lerp and its contained honeydew.
