### 

Innovative Foraging by the House Sparrow *Passer domesticus*

 Neil Shelley
P. 46-47

### Abstract

This note describes an incidental observation of House Sparrows *Passer domesticus* foraging for insects trapped within the engine bay of motor vehicles in South-eastern Australia.
