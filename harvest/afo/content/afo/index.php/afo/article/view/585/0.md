### 

The Eastern Whipbird: A Seed Predator of the Endangered Caley’s Grevillea *Grevillea caleyi*

 CARL R GOSPER, TANYA M LLORENS
p. 99-101

### Abstract

This note describes an observation of an Eastern Whipbird *Psophodes olivaceus* collecting, manipulating and feeding on the seeds of the endangered shrub Caley’s Grevillea* Grevillea caleyi*.
