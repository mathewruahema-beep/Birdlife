### 

A Third Specimen Record and Additional Notes on the Spotted Pardalote *Pardalotus punctatus *in Sunraysia 

 Chris Sonter
pp. 181-184

### Abstract

Observations and a specimen of the Spotted Pardalote *Pard a lotus punctatus *from Mildura (Victoria) in 1985 are presented, and previous local and regional records are reviewed. An influx of the species to Mildura in 1985 appears to be exceptional; records suggest that the species undertakes winter movements that only occasionally reach the Sunraysia district of north-west Victoria.
