### 

A First Breeding Record of the Red-capped Plover *Charadrius ruficapillus* for Wallacea and Notes on Other Waders Observed at Tasi-tolu, Timor-Leste

 Jerry Olsen, Colin R Trainor
p. 1-4

### Abstract

We present photographic evidence of the first breeding record for the Red-capped Plover *Charadrius ruficapillus* in Wallacea, and survey data for waders at the little-explored Tasi-tolu wetlands near Dili, Timor-Leste.
