### 

Sexual Dimorphism in Immature Australian King-Parrots

 B J Lepschi, J E Courtney
P. 301-304

### Abstract

A study of wild and captive birds and museum specimens of the Australian King-Parrot *Alisterus *scapularis revealed sexual dimorphism in the immature plumage. Males have the upper mandible a dull orange-red colour, similar to that of the adult bird, and pale green scapular stripes (smaller than in the adult), whereas females have the upper mandible dull brown and no scapular stripe. Most males have some areas of faint red on the breast and more pronounced red colour on the throat than females, although this is apparently an unreliable character and is difficult to assess. Rose-pink tipping to the underside of the tail feathers, a character of adult females, is also present in immatures of both sexes.
