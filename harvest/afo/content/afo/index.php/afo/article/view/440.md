### 

Double-brooding by the Brown Goshawk *Accipiter fasciatus* in the Northern Territory

 WILLIAM RIDDELL
p. 92-93

### Abstract

A pair of Brown Goshawks *Accipiter fasciatus didimus* nested successfully twice in the one breeding season in Darwin, Northern Territory, in 2009, raising two fledglings on each occasion. After a successful brood in 2010, a second attempt in that season failed.
