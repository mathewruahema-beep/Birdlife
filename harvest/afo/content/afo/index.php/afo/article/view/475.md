### 

Range Extension for the Blue-winged Parrot

 ANDREW LEY, BRIAN TYNAN
p. 87-88

### Abstract

A series of recent records suggests that the winter range of the Blue-winged Parrot *Neophema chrysostoma* extends farther north into western Queensland than has previously been acknowledged.
