### 

Contributions to the Natural History of Australian Peregrine Falcons *Falco peregrinus macropus*: I. Behaviour at Two Victorian Nests

 Lyn J Turner, Peter Lawrence, Gregory V Czechura
P. 3-12

### Abstract

Observations made at two Peregrine Falcon nesting localities over three breeding seasons are reported. These observations are presented according to the major events of the nesting cycle: pre-nesting, incubation, hatching, nestling and fledging periods. Additional information is provided on antagonistic behaviours and food-caching. The observations are consistent with the literature on Peregrine behaviour and, as a result, it is possible to infer the impact of human interference at one of the nesting sites.
