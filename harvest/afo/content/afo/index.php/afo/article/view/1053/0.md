### 

Relative Frequency of Bird Species in the Harvey Irrigation Area, Western Australia

 Eric H Sedgwick
pp. 146-149

### Abstract

During a ten-year survey of the Harvey Irrigation Area (Western Australia), 78 species of birds were recorded. The most frequently recorded species (Australian Raven *Corvus **coronoides *85%, Australian Magpie *Gymnorhina tibicen *81%, Australian Magpie-lark *Grallina **cyanoleuca *73%) are abundant in open pasture. Eight species are regular migrants: Pallid Cuckoo *Cuculus pallidus, *Horsfield's Bronze-Cuckoo *Chrysococcyx basalis, *Shining Bronze Cuckoo *C lucidus, *Sacred Kingfisher *Halcyon sancta, *Rainbow Bee-eater *Merops ornatus, *White-winged Triller *Lalage sueurii, *Rufous Songlark *Cinclorhamphus mathewsi *and Brown Songlark *C cruralis. *Roadside native vegetation is considered to be important in retaining some bird species in the irrigation area.
