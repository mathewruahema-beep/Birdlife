### 

Singing Frequency over the Year of Skylarks and Brown Songlarks at Gatum, Western Victoria

 Joost Brouwer
P. 35-37

### Abstract

Singing frequency and sightings of the Skylark *Alauda arvensis *and Brown Songlark *Cinclorhamphus cruralis *were recorded at Gatum, in western Victoria, from December 1984 to June 1988. Skylarks were present all year but were sometimes silent in late summer-autumn. Songlarks were spring-summer migrants, present but silent in early autumn and mid winter but apparently absent mid autumn-early winter.
