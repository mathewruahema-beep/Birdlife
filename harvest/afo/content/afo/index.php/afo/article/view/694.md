### 

Waterbirds of the Arcoona Lakes, an Important Arid-zone Wetland Complex in South Australia

 J L Read, F R Ebdon
P. 234-244

### Abstract

The Arcoona Lakes are a series of ephemeral freshwater, brackish and saline lakes between Woomera and Roxby Downs in northern South Australia. After flooding rains in 1989, the lakes supported over 150 000 waterbirds, including large numbers of Freckled Ducks *Stictonetta naevosa. *Breeding details of 15 species are presented, including repeated nesting by Black Swans *Cygnus atratus, *Little Pied Cormorants *Phalacrocorax melanoleucos, *Eurasian Coots *Fulica atra *and Gull-billed Terns *Sterna nilotica. *Predators and habitat affinities of waterbirds, along with the regional and national significance of the Arcoona Lakes, are discussed.
