### 

A Nesting Association between the Weebill *Smicrornis brevirostris *and the Spider *Badumna candida*

 J N Hobbs
P. 253-256

### Abstract

A nesting association between the Weebill *Smicrornis brevirostris *and the communal nesting spider *Badumna candida *is described and discussed. Up to 71% of Weebill nests were found in association. Concealment of the Weebill nests is possibly the main purpose. The Yellow-rumped Thornhill *Acanthiza **chrysorrhoa *and Zebra Finch *Poephila guttata *may also nest in association with the spider.
