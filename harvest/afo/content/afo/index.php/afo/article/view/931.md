### 

Barau's Petrel *Pterodroma baraui, *a New Species for Australia

 Mike Carter, Tim Reid, Peter Lansley
P. 39-43

### Abstract

Barau's Petrel *Pterodroma baraui *can now be added to the Australian list. An observation by a group of observers in Victorian waters in February 1987 is documented. This occurrence is discussed in relation to the Indian Ocean distribution of this species and to subsequent observations off Western Australia.
