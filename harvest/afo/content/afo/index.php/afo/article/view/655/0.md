### 

White-winged Triller *Lalage sueurii* Nests Incorporating Faecal Pellets of a Processionary Caterpillar *Ochrogaster lunifer*

 GRAHAM R FULTON
p. 53-54

### Abstract

Two White-winged Triller *Lalage sueurii* nests incorporating faecal pellets of processionary caterpillars *Ochrogaster lunifer*, from south-western Australia, are described. Nest site re-use is also reported.
