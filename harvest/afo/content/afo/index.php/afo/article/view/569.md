### 

Tracking Down the Predator of Radio-tagged Bell Miners *Manorina melanophrys*

 PAUL D MEEK
p. 153-155

### Abstract

Three radio-tagged Bell Miners* Manorina melanophrys* from a study population of 13 tagged birds died or dropped their tags during a study in Kumbatine National Park, New South Wales. One radio-transmitter was located within the scat of a Lace Monitor *Varanus varius*.
