### 

The Breeding Status of Little Tern *Sterna albifrons, *East Gippsland, Victoria 1977-1980

 Joan Vincent
pp. 35-60

### Abstract

A study of breeding sites and breeding success of the Little Tern *Sterna albifrons *extending over three seasons to 1979-80 has been conducted along the East Gippsland coastline. Sites recorded in the literature were investigated and previously unrecorded ones were located. There are few active sites and breeding success is very low. Human disturbance is a major factor and unless an appropriate management plan is prepared and executed the birds will most probably become extinct in Victoria as a breeding species. This study provides sufficient information upon which to base an effective management programme for the conservation of the Little Tern.
