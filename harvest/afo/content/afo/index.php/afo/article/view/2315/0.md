### 

Piscivory in the Comb-crested Jacana *Irediparra gallinacea*

 Matthew Mo, Anthony C. Gill, Amanda Hay

### Abstract

This note describes two observations of fish predation by Comb-crested Jacana(s) *Irediparra gallinacea* at the Cattai Wetlands, Coopernook, New South Wales. The fish were ~3 cm and 5–6 cm long, and one was identified as an Empire Gudgeon *Hypseleotris compressa*. These observations appear to be the first reported instances of Comb-crested Jacana(s) handling and consuming fish prey, with the only previous study reporting piscivory limited to identifying trace remains of fish in
 stomach contents.
