### 

Inland Records of the Bell Miner in Northern New South Wales

 R A WATTS, A ASHWORTH
p. 105-106

### Abstract

A small group of about four Bell Miners *Manorina melanocephala* occurred at Dungowan, ~20 km south-east of Tamworth on the North-west Slopes of New South Wales, throughout at least February–May 2009. This occurrence followed an earlier, temporary one at Ogunbil, on the North-west Slopes ~20 km south-east of Dungowan, in the 1980s.
