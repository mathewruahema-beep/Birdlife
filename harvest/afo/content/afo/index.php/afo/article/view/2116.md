### 

Pied Currawong *Strepera graculina* nesting in an underground carpark

 Graham R. Fulton
pp. 91-92

### Abstract

A pair of Pied Currawongs *Strepera graculina* was observed nesting in an underground carpark of a suburban shopping centre in Brisbane, Queensland. Previous years’ nests, presumed to be from this species, were detected,
indicating that these birds had probably been doing this for at least 3 years. Such plasticity in nest placement suggests that this species is continuing to adjust to urban environs, which might lead to increasing its already significant nestpredatory impact on other birds.
