### 

Mixed Honeyeater Flocks in Northern Queensland

 Bryan Sage
P. 260-264

### Abstract

Mixed-species flocks of honeyeaters (Meliphagidae) were observed at two sites in northern Queensland in June 1992. The first record concerned six species of six different genera in the Gregory River area, and the second ten species from seven different genera on Cape York Peninsula. The first record of Yellow-throated Miner *Manorina flavigula *for Cape York Peninsula is presented.
