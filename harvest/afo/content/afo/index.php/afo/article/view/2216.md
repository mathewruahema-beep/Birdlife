### 

Remains of European Wasps *Vespula germanica* detected in pellets of Pied Currawong *Strepera graculina*

 Naomi Monk

### Abstract

This observation of two regurgitated pellets deposited by Pied Currawongs *Strepera graculina*, in Tawonga, north-eastern Victoria, indicates targeted predation on the invasive European Wasp *Vespula germanica*.
