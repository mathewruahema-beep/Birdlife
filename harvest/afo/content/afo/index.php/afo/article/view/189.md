### 

The Effects of an Oil Spill at Apollo Bay, Victoria, on Little Penguins *Eudyptula minor* in May 1990

 R Jessop, P Du Guesclin
P. 192-198

### Abstract

On 21 May 1990, an oil spill was observed off the southern Victorian coast, between Apollo Bay and Lorne. The first oiled Little Penguin *Eudyptula minor* came ashore the following day. Overall, 327 oiled birds of 6 species, including 318 Little Penguins, were recovered. The percentage of Penguins rehabilitated and released from Apollo Bay was 38.7%. This is considered low, and was attributable to a number of factors, which are discussed.
