### 

Variation in the Male Whip-crack of the Eastern Whipbird *Psophodes olivaceus*

 Wendy R Davidson, Naomi E Langmore
P. 82-84

### Abstract

Recordings of the song of the Eastern Whipbird *Psophodes olivaceus *in Dorrigo National Park, New South Wales, reveal variation in the male whip-crack component of the song, previously regarded as being constant. As well as the standard rising whip-crack, at least one individual uttered a falling whip-crack. Another individual uttered a combination of these types, producing a 'down up' whip-crack.
