### 

Christmas Island Goshawk predation on Abbott’s Booby chick and competition with a Robber Crab

 Janos C Hennicke
p. 196-200

### Abstract

The Christmas Island Goshawk *Accipiter hiogaster natalis* is endemic to Christmas Island, Indian Ocean. Little is known of its ecology. Here, for the first time, predation by a Christmas Island Goshawk on an Abbott’s Booby *Papasula abbotti* nestling is described, as is the ensuing tug of war between the Goshawk and a Robber Crab* Birgus latro*. The observations provide insights into the hunting behaviour, prey value, and food competition of this scarcely known raptor.
