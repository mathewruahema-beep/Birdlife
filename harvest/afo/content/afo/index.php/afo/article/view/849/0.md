### 

Observations on Birds in Snow Gum Woodland and Alpine Heathland during Mid Winter in South-eastern Australia

 Martin Schultz
P. 95-102

### Abstract

This paper presents a summary of incidental observations of birds recorded during the mid-winter period in Snow Gum woodland and alpine heathland in ten areas of south-eastern Australia. From a total of ninety-three days spent in the snow-covered environment in July and August over a five-year period forty-three species of birds were recorded. Six species are regarded as common, nineteen species as uncommon and eighteen species as rare. Incidental observations were made of the roosting and foraging behaviour and habitat preference of species, where available.
