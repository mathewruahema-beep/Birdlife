### 

A Record of the Long-tailed Jaeger *Stercorarius longicaudus* in the Australian Capital Territory

 Walter E Boles, A B Rose, Jerry Olsen
P. 158-162

### Abstract

Remains of a Long-tailed Jaeger *Stercorarius longicaudus* at the nest of a Peregrine Falcon *Falco peregrinus* at Corin Dam comprise the first record of this species from the inland section of the Australian Capital Territory. Osteological criteria used for making this identification are presented.
