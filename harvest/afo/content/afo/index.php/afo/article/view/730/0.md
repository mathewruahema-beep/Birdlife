### 

Captive Studies Reveal Extensive Vocal Range in the Sacred Kingfisher

 Karin Sitte, Darryl Jones
P. 298-305

### Abstract

While rehabilitating Sacred Kingfishers *Halcyon sancta *following injury, we recorded many vocalisations previously undescribed and possibly unsuspected. This paper describes eight new calls in two groups: loud calls given primarily during the day, and softer calls produced mainly during the night in a social context. It is suggested that these calls reflect a much greater level of vocal behaviour for this species than previously expected.
