### 

Brown Goshawk *Accipiter fasciatus* Attacking a Green Pygmy-goose *Nettapus pulchellus* over and in Water

 Matthew J Ward, James G Smith
P. 105-106

### Abstract

This note describes an attack by a Brown Goshawk *Accipiter fasciatus* on a Green Pygmy-goose *Nettapus pulchellus*, involving possible mediated flushing of the Pygmy-goose, and ending with Goshawk and prey in the water and prey escaping.
