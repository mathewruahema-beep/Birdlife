### 

Rainbow Lorikeets Nesting Below Ground Level

 RUTH BRIGHT
p. 86-87

### Abstract

In suburban Sydney, New South Wales, a pair of Rainbow Lorikeets *Trichoglossus haematodus* nested for three consecutive years in an underground hollow beneath a cut-off tree-stump.
