### 

The Birds of the Iron Range Area of Cape York Peninsula

 H R Johnson, N Hooper
pp. 80-95

### Abstract

An account of the birds that were observed by the members of the Bird Observers' Club during a campout at Iron Range, Cape York Peninsula, between August 2 and 19, 1970, with accompanying map.
