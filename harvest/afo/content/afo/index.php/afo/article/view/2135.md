### 

Additions to prey taken by Wedge-tailed Eagles *Aquila audax* after release of Rabbit *Oryctolagus cuniculus* haemorrhagic disease (Rabbit Calicivirus) in 1996

 Graham R Fulton
pp. 11-12

### Abstract

Identifications of five species of birds considered prey of Wedge-tailed Eagles *Aquila audax* following the 1996 release of Rabbit *Oryctolagus cuniculus* haemorrhagic disease (Rabbit Calicivirus) in western New South Wales are given. The following taxa were identified from their sterna in Wedge-tailed Eagle nests: Sulphur-crested Cockatoo* Cacatua galerita*, Galah *Eolophus roseicapilla*, Australian Magpie *Gymnorhina tibicen*, Little Crow *Corvus bennetti* and Plains-wanderer *Pedionomus torquatus*.
