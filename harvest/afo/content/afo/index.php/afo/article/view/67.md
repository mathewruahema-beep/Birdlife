### 

Wedge-tailed Eagle preying on Purple Swamphen

 Adam N Smith
pp. 157-159

### Abstract

This note documents an observed capture of a Purple Swamphen* Porphyrio porphyrio* by a Wedge-tailed Eagle *Aquila audax*, at a wetland in north-eastern New South Wales. Such events are rarely observed or described.
