### 

Attentiveness, Parental Attachment and Predation at a Nest of the Silvereye *Zosterops lateralis familiaris* in Wollongong, New South Wales

 K A Wood
P. 254-259

### Abstract

A nest of the Silvereye *Zosterops lateralis familiaris*, with two nestlings about eight days old, was observed with a telescope during four watches over two days. The overall brooding constancy was 34% and the mean feeding rate was 3. 7 meals per nestling per hour. The duration of feeding visits ranged from five to 110 seconds (median 10 seconds). Parent birds showed attachment to the nest after the nestlings were presumed to have been taken by a nest predator. The nest was removed, analysed in detail and compared with the construction of four other Mangerton nests from previous years.
