### 

Vocal Mimicry of Larger Honeyeaters by the Regent Honeyeater *Xanthomya phrygia*

 Philip A Veerman
P. 180-189

### Abstract

This paper presents evidence of solitary Regent Honeyeaters *Xanthomyza phrygia *associating with a variety of larger honeyeaters and mimicking the calls of those other species. The significance of this behaviour is discussed along with a summary of other aspects of the Regent Honeyeater's vocal behaviour. It may be a unique case of vocal *mimicry *used for deception.
