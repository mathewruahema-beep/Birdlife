### 

Cinnamon Bittern *Ixobrychus cinnamomeus* on Christmas Island: A New Bird for Australian Territory

 Mike Carter
P. 55-58

### Abstract

This is an account of the first Australian record of the Cinnamon Bittern *Ixobrychus cinnamomeus*. The bird, an adult female, died soon after being found alive but in an emaciated condition. The specimen, with the registration number B24113, is now retained in Museum Victoria.
