### 

Observations on a Breeding Pair of Collared Sparrowhawks *Accipiter cirrhocephalus*

 David Hollands
P. 200-203

### Abstract

A pair of Collared Sparrow hawks *Accipiter cirrhocephalus *was watched for 9.3 hours during the incubation period, October/November 1984, at Orbost, Victoria. After approximately 35 days' incubation of the clutch of five eggs, the nest failed. The female incubated for 89%, and the male for 11%, of observation time. Sex roles, behaviour and calls are described.
