### 

Brown Falcon *Falco berigora *Pair Successfully Raises Australian Hobby *F longipennis *Nestlings

 Paul G McDonald
P. 40-44

### Abstract

During a long-term study of the behaviour and ecology of the Brown Falcon *Falco berigora *in southern Victoria, a pair was observed successfully raising Australian Hobby *F. longipenni *nestlings. This unusual observation is most likely a consequence of only a single nest-site being available within the territory of both pairs, with the Brown Falcon pair taking over the nest and eggs after the Hobby pair had laid.
