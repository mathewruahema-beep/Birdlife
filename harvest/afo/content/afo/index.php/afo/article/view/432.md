### 

Apparent Attempted Predation on a Tree-frog by a Little Shrike-thrush

 James BAIRD
p. 51-52

### Abstract

This note describes an apparent predation attempt by a Little Shrike-thrush *Colluricincla megarhyncha* on an Orange-thighed Tree-Frog *Litoria xanthocera*. The frog was dislodged as the bird foraged in a palm tree and, although the bird followed it to the ground, the frog escaped into nearby cover.
