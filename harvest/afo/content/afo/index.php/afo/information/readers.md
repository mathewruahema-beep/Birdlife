###### 

*Australian Field Ornithology* is an on-line annual peer reviewed journal, including an annual volume index. Articles will be made available online soon after acceptance and members will be informed of new articles via BirdLife Australia's monthly e-news and occasional direct emails. **All articles are open access.**

If you are having difficulties accessing AFO Online please contact us by phone or email - afo@birdlife.org.au
