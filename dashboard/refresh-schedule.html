Two routines are needed because the window crosses UTC midnight and one routine holds one cron. Every refresh layer now opens with a cheap freshness check (one Salesforce aggregate + one Asana probe) and stops there when the queue hasn’t moved — so a quiet slot costs a few percent of a full rebuild. The October daylight-saving one-shot has been rewritten to shift all four pieces to AEDT automatically on 5 Oct.

One manual step required The two new hourly routines cannot read Salesforce or Asana until you attach connectors — the platform doesn’t allow attaching them via API. One-time fix on your phone or desktop: claude.ai → Routines → each “hourly refresh” routine → add Salesforce Production + Asana. (The same known gap exists on the 7am daily job.) Until then, the 10-minute live loop is doing the real refreshing.

These runs draw on the claude.ai subscription’s usage allowance rather than billing per token — the dollar figures are the equivalent API price, to show scale. After the halving this sits at roughly half the original hourly build’s cost, often less, because unchanged slots stop after one cheap query. Want it lower still: drop the 8am slot or stretch to 3-hourly.
