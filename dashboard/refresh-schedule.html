Two routines are needed for the hourly window because 6am–4pm AEST crosses UTC midnight and one routine holds one cron. The October daylight-saving one-shot has been rewritten to shift all four pieces to AEDT automatically on 5 Oct.

One manual step required The two new hourly routines cannot read Salesforce or Asana until you attach connectors — the platform doesn’t allow attaching them via API. One-time fix on your phone or desktop: claude.ai → Routines → each “hourly refresh” routine → add Salesforce Production + Asana. (The same known gap exists on the 7am daily job.) Until then, the 10-minute live loop is doing the real refreshing.

These runs draw on the claude.ai subscription’s usage allowance rather than billing per token — the dollar figures are the equivalent API price, to show scale. The hourly schedule is roughly 11× the previous daily cost; the 10-minute loop, when active, adds roughly 6× an hourly run per hour. Cheapest levers if it’s too much: drop the loop to 30 min, or trim the hourly window (e.g. 8am–2pm).
