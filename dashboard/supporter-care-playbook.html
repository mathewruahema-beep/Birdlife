Three findings drive everything on this page:

  • ~44% of closures involve no substantive supporter outcome — no-action, manually-sent autoreplies, spam, duplicates, abandoned. Another 29% close by sending a template email.
  • The team can't see its own workload. 10,719 of 14,757 cases carry no Sub Type, so the tagged process volumes below are floors.
  • The top repeatable process is Update Contact Details — 674/yr tagged (realistically 2–3×), fully deterministic, entirely inside Salesforce, and its closing step is already automated by the live "Auto close case" flow.
Where things stand: the automation is designed and built as deployable metadata on branch claude/supporter-care-automation-design-2epbbp — three flows, all Draft status, so nothing can execute. How a supporter-care case moves today

Supporter Care runs on the General Enquiry Case record type. One assignment rule routes inbound cases to queues — Supporter Care Membership Regular Giving eStore Support and 30 others — and a live flow stamps the record type from the owning queue.

Intake channels (last 365 days)

New ─▶ In Progress ─▶ Pending / On Hold-Internal / Waiting-External ─▶ Response Received ─▶ Closed

Closing is reason-driven and already semi-automated. Case_Closed_Reason__c is mandatory (validation rule); the active flow Auto close case flips Status to Closed the moment a reason is saved. Everything between "case created" and "reason saved" is manual — production has no acknowledgement, triage or resolution automation on these cases.

The automation — click each stage for how / why / impact

Pattern: Triage-and-Guided-Resolve. Three stages; the third already exists in production and is reused untouched.

Deliberately not in this design: no writing of supporter data straight from email content; no auto-closing anything a human hasn't approved; no changes to assignment rules, queues, record types or existing flows; deceased notices excluded; no AI components in phase 1. Licence impact: zero — the org is at 70/70 and this consumes none.

What the calculator can't count

  • Instant acknowledgement for every email/web supporter — the 524-case "New" backlog stops being a supporter-visible problem on day one, regardless of resolution speed.
  • The 73% untagged problem shrinks — every flow-resolved case is tagged, and intake classification proposes tags on the rest. In a year you get a truthful workload picture, which is what picks automation #2 and #3.
  • Risk down: addresses go through NPSP correctly every time; recurring-giving and duplicate warnings appear at the moment of edit, not after the next mail-out bounces.
  • A reusable pattern for Update Recurring Payment (497/yr), Membership Renewal (430/yr), Receipt resend (post-Conga) and Unsubscribe (174/yr).
