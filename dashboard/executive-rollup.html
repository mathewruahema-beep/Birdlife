Reading this page

Each card is the department's control number, not a summary of everything — the dashboard behind it carries the scope captions and the data-quality caveats. Positives are stated alongside defects by design: campaign attribution is 100%, Stripe payouts are all paid and reconciled, membership income is tracking level, and refund volume is quiet. The two structural asks that unblock the most panels: authorise the NetSuite connector (Finance, People, Conservation financials) and publish the drafted unreconciled-income exception Zap.
Provenance. Live tiles: read-only SOQL and Stripe reads, 13 Aug 2026 (AEST). Definitions: insights/ on the Birdlife repo. Dashboards are point-in-time until a refresh routine is attached — the recommended pattern is folding a weekly refresh into the existing weekday ICT routine rather than adding new scheduled jobs.
