The governance loop

Every change to the estate, from creating a routine to teaching a skill a new gotcha, moves through the same four steps.

STEP 1

Propose

The exact change: name, cron in UTC and AEST, prompt, connectors, and which existing job absorbs or is absorbed.

STEP 2

Approve

Mathew gives the go-ahead. Per-session relaxations honoured, same as the charter.

STEP 3

Execute + verify

Make the change, then prove it took: next fire time sane, connectors attached, a paused routine actually stopped.

STEP 4

Record

Register updated, retired definitions backed up to routines/, committed and pushed in the same session.
